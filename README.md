# Road Accident Fatalities in India, 2020–2023

## Question

How did reported road-accident fatalities change across Indian States and Union Territories from 2020 to 2023? Which regions had the largest increases and decreases?

## Data and method

I compared State/UT fatality counts for 2020 and 2023. Ladakh is excluded from both national totals because its 2020 value is missing; its 2023 count is 59. I calculated percentage change as:

(2023 fatalities − 2020 fatalities) ÷ 2020 fatalities × 100

City-level figures are a separate 2023 snapshot. They are not included in the State/UT trend calculation.

## Findings

The comparable State/UT total increased by **24.89%** between 2020 and 2023.

Uttar Pradesh had the largest increase in fatalities by count: **4,503**, from 19,149 in 2020 to 23,652 in 2023.

Manipur had the largest decrease by count: **54**, from 127 to 73.

Mizoram had the largest percentage increase: **128.6%**, from 42 to 96. The percentage is large partly because the 2020 baseline was small.

Manipur had the largest percentage decrease: **42.5%**, from 127 to 73.

Among the 53 cities reported by NCRB, Delhi City had the most road-accident cases in 2023, with **5,715**, and the most road-accident deaths, with **1,457**. These city figures cover 2023 only.

## Limitations

These are reported counts. They do not adjust for population, traffic volume, or distance traveled, so they cannot establish which State/UT or city had the highest underlying risk. City figures cover 2023 only and should not be compared with the 2020–2023 State/UT trend.
